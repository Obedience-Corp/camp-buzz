# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. We
will act with empathy, respect, and professionalism.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and welcoming.
- Respect differing viewpoints and accept responsibility for mistakes.
- Focus discussion on what is best for users and the community.
- Keep private information, credentials, and security reports private.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. This
policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
